Super Rangeela - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Super Rangeela handles information when you use the Super Rangeela game.

1. About Super Rangeela

Super Rangeela is an offline game developed as an independent game project. The game is distributed as a paid game through supported app stores.

2. Information We Collect

Super Rangeela does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photographs, or precise location through the game.

The game does not require users to submit personal information in order to play.

3. Game Data Stored on Your Device

Super Rangeela stores certain game-related information locally on your device so that game features can work correctly.

This may include:

  • High score
  • Game progress used for resume functionality
  • Win count
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Tutorial/How To Play status

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted by the game to a developer-operated server.

4. Internet and Offline Gameplay

Super Rangeela is designed as an offline game. Core gameplay does not require an internet connection, and the game does not send gameplay progress or locally stored game data to the developer's servers.

5. Advertising

Super Rangeela does not display advertisements in the current release.

6. Analytics and Tracking

Super Rangeela does not use the game to directly collect or transmit personal information for advertising or user-tracking purposes.

7. Payments

Super Rangeela is distributed as a paid game. Payments for purchasing the game are processed by the applicable app store or platform.

Super Rangeela does not receive or store users' payment card, banking, or other payment credentials.

8. Third-Party Services

The current release of Super Rangeela does not require users to create an account with a third-party service in order to play the game.

If third-party services are introduced in a future version and they change the way user or device information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Game-related information stored locally remains on the user's device until it is removed by the game, overwritten through normal gameplay, or deleted by the user by removing the game's local data or uninstalling the game, subject to the operating system's behavior.

Because Super Rangeela does not maintain a developer-operated account or server containing users' personal information, there is no developer-side personal account data to request for deletion.

10. Children's Privacy

Super Rangeela does not knowingly request personal information from children. The game does not require users to provide personal information to play.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game's features, data practices, or distribution platforms change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
